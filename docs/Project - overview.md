# SGUC Project Overview

## 1. Project Name

**Sidra Global Utility Card (SGUC)**

## 2. Project Overview

SGUC (Sidra Global Utility Card) is a fully digital utility and commerce platform designed to connect users, businesses, merchants, and essential digital services within one unified ecosystem.

SGUC enables users to access supported everyday services, make supported digital payments, save through discounts, receive cashback, and earn reward points from eligible transactions.

The platform operates as a virtual utility card integrated into a mobile application and web platform.

### Core Value

**Pay → Save → Cashback → Earn → Reward**

The objective is to make everyday digital transactions more valuable for users while helping businesses increase visibility, engagement, and customer loyalty.

## 3. Sharia Compliance

SGUC is designed around Sharia-compliant and ethical commerce principles.

The platform aims to ensure that:

- Transactions are free from interest (riba)
- Prohibited activities are avoided
- Business partnerships follow ethical requirements
- Marketplace offerings are screened for compliance
- Rewards and cashback structures are designed around compliant digital commerce

SGUC aims to provide users and partner businesses with a trusted and ethically responsible digital ecosystem.
