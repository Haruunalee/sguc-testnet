# SGUC Vision

## The Commerce Internet

SGUC (Sidra Global Utility Card) is a fully digital utility and commerce platform designed to connect users, businesses, merchants, and essential digital services within one unified ecosystem.

SGUC enables users to access supported services, make supported digital payments, save through discounts, receive cashback, and earn rewards from eligible transactions.

**The Commerce Internet — Connecting Every Business, Everywhere.**

## Core Value

SGUC is built around a simple principle:

**Pay → Save → Cashback → Earn → Reward**

The goal is not only to make digital transactions easier, but to make supported everyday transactions more valuable for users and businesses.

## Core Services

SGUC's initial ecosystem focuses on essential digital services, including:

- Food & Food Delivery
- Transport & Ride-hailing
- Airtime & Data
- Hotels & Travel
- E-commerce & Online Shopping
- Global eSIM & Connectivity
- Online Services & Digital Subscriptions

These are the initial focus areas and are not intended to limit the future expansion of the SGUC ecosystem.

SGUC is designed to support additional digital and real-world services through future merchant, business, platform, and API integrations.

## Payments

SGUC is designed to provide supported digital payment solutions across participating services and partner platforms.

Eligible users may receive applicable:

- Discounts
- Cashback
- Reward Points
- Merchant Offers
- Loyalty Benefits

on eligible transactions.

The payment ecosystem is designed to expand over time as new services, merchants, partners, and integrations are added.

## SGUC Rewards & Savings

The SGUC rewards ecosystem is designed to turn everyday transactions into additional value.

### Initial Rewards Categories

During the initial ecosystem phase, SGUC will prioritize discounts, cashback, reward points, and exclusive offers across:

- Food & Food Delivery
- Transport & Ride-hailing
- Airtime & Data
- Hotels & Travel
- E-commerce & Online Shopping
- Global eSIM & Connectivity

Additional services may be added to the rewards ecosystem as partnerships, integrations, market demand, and operational capabilities expand.

Users can potentially:

- Save through discounts
- Receive cashback
- Earn reward points
- Access exclusive merchant offers
- Unlock additional benefits through future loyalty features

The objective is to create a continuous value cycle:

**Pay → Save → Earn → Reward → Use Again**

## Merchant & Business Ecosystem

SGUC is designed not only for consumers, but also for businesses and merchants.

Participating businesses may benefit from:

- Increased digital visibility
- Customer engagement
- Merchant discounts
- Reward campaigns
- Cashback campaigns
- Digital promotions
- Marketplace integrations
- API-based commerce integrations

This creates a two-sided ecosystem connecting:

**Users + Merchants + Businesses + Digital Services**

## Sidra Chain — Initial Blockchain Ecosystem

SGUC's blockchain ecosystem development begins with Sidra Chain.

The initial objective is to explore and develop suitable SGUC blockchain infrastructure around the Sidra ecosystem, subject to technical requirements, network capabilities, compliance requirements, and applicable ecosystem policies.

Blockchain technology may support future SGUC capabilities including:

- Digital asset infrastructure
- Reward systems
- Payment-related utilities
- Merchant ecosystem utilities
- Transparent transaction-related records where appropriate
- Future blockchain-based commerce features

Sensitive personal and commercial information should not be placed on-chain unnecessarily.

## Sharia Compliance

SGUC is designed around Sharia-compliant and ethical commerce principles.

The platform aims to avoid:

- Interest (riba)
- Haram activities
- Non-compliant business partnerships
- Prohibited products and services

Marketplace and business partners are intended to be screened according to SGUC's compliance requirements.

## Future Ecosystem Expansion

SGUC is designed as an expandable digital utility ecosystem rather than a platform limited to a fixed number of services.

Future categories may include:

- Utility & Bill Payments
- Internet Services
- Education Services
- Events & Tickets
- Entertainment & Digital Subscriptions
- Travel & Flights
- Additional Merchant Services
- Government & Utility Integrations
- Other supported digital services

Future services will depend on technical feasibility, partnerships, regulatory requirements, market demand, and compliance standards.

The long-term platform may include:

- Multi-Currency Support
- Contactless Payments

- AI Spending & Savings Insights
- Reward Management

- Expanded Payment Integrations

## Future Pi Network Integration

SGUC may pursue integration with the Pi ecosystem as part of its future multi-ecosystem expansion strategy.

Any Pi integration will be subject to:

- Pi Network technical requirements
